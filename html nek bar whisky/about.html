"The Legacy of Lagavulin Distillery"

Located on the windswept shores of Islay, Lagavulin Distillery is renowned for its deep, smoky, and complex single malts. Established in 1816, it has become an icon of the Islay whisky tradition, producing rich peated drams beloved by whisky enthusiasts worldwide.

During our event, you will step into the historic warehouses, breathe in the aroma of aging whisky casks, and witness the craftsmanship behind Lagavulin’s legendary expressions.

Paired with an exclusive horseback tour, this experience offers a rare opportunity to embrace both the natural beauty and the whisky heritage of Islay in a truly immersive way.
